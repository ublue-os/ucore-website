**All uCore streams are now on Fedora CoreOS 45!**
Testing moved on November 7; stable and LTS followed on November 20 with Fedora CoreOS 45.20261030.3.1.

Stable and testing continue to follow their respective Fedora CoreOS kernels, and LTS continues to use the longterm 6.18 kernel. Note that new builds of testing are currently blocked on kernel ZFS failing to build with kernel 7.3.

mergerfs is currently using the Fedora 44 build, temporarily, until the Fedora 45 packages officially releases. Also a targeted SELinux policy fix is applied on affected Fedora 45 bases. See [PR #467](https://github.com/ublue-os/ucore/pull/467) for details.

Existing systems will see the changes through their usual update process. See [Choosing an image](/docs/images/) for stream details and [Existing systems and rebasing](/docs/rebasing/) for switching images.
